FAQ

Common questions

What is a 360° virtual tour?

An interactive online walkthrough of a property. Viewers look around each room as if they’re standing in it, and move from room to room. Good for rental listings, Airbnbs, and homes for sale.

How long does it take?

Most properties take under an hour to capture. Your completed tour link is delivered within 48 hours.

Do I need any equipment or software?

No. We handle capture, editing, and hosting, and you get a simple link that works in any browser — nothing to install.

Can you do a whole building?

Yes — that’s the job we do most. We shot an entire apartment community in Mankato: the property plus fifteen individual units, each with its own shareable link. See all sixteen tours.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Mankato and most of our work is around Minnesota — Minneapolis, St. Paul, Rochester, Duluth and in between. We travel further for larger jobs, so if you’re outside the state just ask.
