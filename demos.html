What it took

The honest version

Three buildings, one visit each

East, West and C. Units were shot as they became available between tenants, so the job ran across several sessions rather than one long day.

Under an hour per unit

Capture is quick. The time goes into editing and linking the rooms together so a viewer can actually walk the place.

Sixteen separate links

Not one tour with sixteen sections. Each unit stands alone, so the leasing team can paste a single unit into a single listing.
